The day this stopped being just two people talking and became something we wanted to take seriously.

A small reminder

We are allowed to disagree.

We can be angry. We can be hurt. We can need space. We can even think the other person is being an absolute idiot.

But a difficult moment doesn't get to rewrite every beautiful moment that came before it.

You and me are on the same team.

Even when we're temporarily terrible at acting like it.

When we're both too stubborn

Our tiny rules.

1

Don't fight to win.
Fight to understand what hurt the other person.

2

Don't weaponise love.
Being upset shouldn't become a reason to make the other person feel unwanted.

3

Take a breath.
Sometimes “I need a little time” is better than ten messages we don't really mean.

4

Come back.
Space is okay. Disappearing from the relationship isn't the point.

5

Remember the intention.
We chose each other because we wanted to build something, not because we'd never disagree.

For my favourite person

One last thing.

I don't need us to be perfect. I just want us to keep choosing to understand each other.

A face I would rather see smiling than across an argument.
